AquaGrid: Water Distribution Network Planner

An AI planner that finds the cheapest flow path from reservoir to demand zone, isolates leaking pipes and schedules valves so pressure targets are met. Pick an algorithm, click a zone to make it the goal, click a pipe to isolate it as a leak.

Ask the AquaGrid agent

The agent answers engineers' questions by calling the planner's tools: search, algorithm comparison, heuristic checks, valve optimisation and leak isolation. Isolating a pipe is a risky action, so you approve or reject it as the human reviewer. The map above updates as it works.

Heuristic evaluation

Each heuristic is compared with the true cost-to-goal h* at every reachable node. Admissible means it never overestimates.

Simulated annealing schedules valves cheaper than a fixed setting

Six zone valves are set to closed, ⅓, ⅔ or full for each of twelve two-hour slots. The cost adds unmet demand, over-pressure leakage, pumping energy at the slot's tariff, pump-capacity overruns and valve switching. Hill climbing and simulated annealing start from the same random schedule; the naive baseline keeps every valve at ⅔.
